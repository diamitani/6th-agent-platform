## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

### Primary ICP — The Emerging Independent Artist

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Independent musician/producer/songwriter |
| Age | 22–35 |
| Followers | 1K–100K on Spotify/IG/TikTok |
| Income | $0–$50K from music; has a day job |
| Location | US, UK, Canada (English-first) |
| Pain | Doing everything alone; losing money to bad deals; no team |
| Trigger | Getting a booking inquiry or label interest with no infrastructure |
| Watering Holes | r/WeAreTheMusicMakers, r/edmproduction, SubmitHub, Groover, Discord music servers, TikTok creator spaces, YouTube comments on music business channels |
| Platforms | Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Spotify |

### Secondary ICP — The Hustling Producer

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Who | Beat maker, sound designer, session musician |
| Age | 18–30 |
| Pain | No idea how to register beats, license music, or build a brand |
| Trigger | First sync deal inquiry or YouTube Content ID claim |

### What They Say (Verbatim)

> "I don't know how to register my music with a PRO."  
> "Someone wants to book me but I don't have a contract."  
> "My music is being used without credit — what do I do?"  
> "I don't know if I need an LLC or EIN."  
> "I need a professional EPK but I don't know what goes in it."

## 🔄 Product Update Protocol

### Every Product Update Follows This Sequence

```
ANNOUNCE → EXECUTE → SHIP → FOLLOW UP

1. ANNOUNCE (before building):
   "Building [feature]. Goes live [date]. Here's why it matters."
   Channels: LinkedIn (Pat), IG Story, Email list

2. EXECUTE (silent sprint):
   No public updates until shipped.
   Internal: Chief of Staff tracks progress.

3. SHIP (launch day):
   "It's live. Here's what it does. Here's the link."
   Channels: All social + Email blast + DM warm users

4. FOLLOW UP (3 days later):
   "Here's who's using it and what they said."
   Channels: IG post + LinkedIn + Email
```
