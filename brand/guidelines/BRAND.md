## 2. Logo

### Primary Mark — "6A" The Eye

The 6thAgent logo is a lettermark symbol: the **"6A" eye**. Inspired by:
- The **CBS eye** — iconic, simple, one continuous form
- The **Eye of Horus** — ancient symbol of protection, royal power, and good health
- The **All-Seeing Eye** — awareness, vision, championship focus
- A **championship ring** — circular, eternal, victory

**Meaning:**
- The **"6"** curves around to form the eye socket / championship ring — infinite, complete, always watching
- The **"A"** forms the pupil — sharp, forward-pointing, focused on the goal
- The **orange glow** — fire, energy, the spark off the bench
- The **negative space** between 6 and A — the "eye" that sees the winning play

### Logo Variations

| File | Usage |
|------|-------|
| `logo-6a-icon.svg` | Primary icon — app icon, favicon, avatar |
| `logo-horizontal.svg` | Horizontal lockup — headers, website nav, business cards |
| `logo-vertical.svg` | Vertical lockup — social media, mobile splash |
| `logo-icon-white.svg` | Reversed for dark backgrounds |
| `logo-icon-black.svg` | Monochrome for print |

### Logo Construction
- **Icon size**: 400×400 viewBox
- **Horizontal lockup**: 600×160 — icon + wordmark + tagline
- **Clear space**: Minimum 1× icon height around all sides
- **Minimum size**: 24px icon / 120px horizontal

### Logo Don'ts
- ❌ Do not stretch or distort
- ❌ Do not change the orange color
- ❌ Do not add shadows, glows, or effects
- ❌ Do not place on busy backgrounds
- ❌ Do not rotate or tilt
- ❌ Do not separate the 6 and A

## 3. Color Palette

### Primary: Champion Orange

The color orange was chosen because:
- **Basketball** — NBA orange, energy, court fire
- **The ball** — the game, the play, the shot
- **Fire** — passion, heat, intensity
- **Sunrise** — new day, new opportunity
- **Visibility** — you can't miss the sixth man on the court

## 8. The "6thAgent" Name

### Etymology
- **"6th"** — The sixth man. In basketball, the first player off the bench. Not a starter, but changes the game. The energy. The spark. The difference between a good team and a champion team.
- **"Agent"** — An AI agent. Autonomous. Persistent. Always working.

### Name Rules
- **Always capitalize**: 6thAgent (never "6thagent" or "6th agent")
- **Logo mark**: "6A" — always uppercase
- **In text**: "6thAgent" (camelCase)
- **Domain**: 6thagent.ai or 6thagent.com
- **Handle**: @6thagent

### Why Not "7th" or "8th"?
The 6th man is the most important role in team sports. Not the star (1-5), but the X-factor. The one who comes in and changes momentum. That's what this platform does for your business.
